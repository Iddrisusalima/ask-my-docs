ANSWER
==========================================================================
Training happened once in the past and is finished, involving feeding the model enormous amounts
of text over months and computing power to adjust billions of internal numbers (weights) until
it predicts the next token well [1][3]. In contrast, inference is what happens when you use the
model: nothing learns or is saved, your prompt is split into tokens and pushed through the
frozen weights once, and the model emits the single most likely next token [4][5].
