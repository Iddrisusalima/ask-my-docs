ANSWER
==========================================================================
Training happened once in the past and is finished, involving feeding the model enormous amounts
of text over months and computing power to adjust billions of internal weights until it became
good at predicting the next token [1][3]. Inference is what happens when you use the model:
nothing learns or is saved, and the prompt is pushed through the frozen weights once to emit the
most likely next token [4][5].
